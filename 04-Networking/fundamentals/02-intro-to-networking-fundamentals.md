# Introduction to Networking Fundamentals

- Chapter 1 introduces computer networks and their role in modern infrastructure and DevOps.

## Topics covered

- Network basics and types:
  - LAN (Local Area Network).
  - WAN (Wide Area Network).
- Key networking components:
  - Routers.
  - Switches.
  - Firewalls.
- IP addressing:
  - IPv4 and IPv6.
  - Why both are used and their importance.
- MAC addresses.
- Ports and protocols.

## What to remember

- This lesson is a chapter overview; each topic will be explained later.
- DevOps relevance: understanding how systems communicate helps you configure connectivity and troubleshoot failures.
