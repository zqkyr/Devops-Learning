# 04-lan-and-wan

## LAN (Local Area Network)

- Connects devices within a limited area, such as a home, office or building.
- Can use wired Ethernet, wireless Wi-Fi, or both.
- Enables local communication and resource sharing.
- Example: sending a document from your laptop to a printer on your home network.
- Devices can communicate locally without internet access.
- A LAN can connect to the internet through a router.

## WAN (Wide Area Network)

- Connects networks over larger geographical areas.
- Enables communication between LANs in different locations.
- Example: connecting a company's London and Manchester office networks.
- The internet is a global network of interconnected networks and a familiar example of WAN-scale connectivity.

## What to remember

- LAN = local connectivity.
- WAN = connectivity between networks over larger distances.
- DevOps relevance: distinguish local connectivity problems from problems reaching remote servers or cloud services.
